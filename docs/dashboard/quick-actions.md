# Dashboard Quick Actions

This guide explains how to use Quick Actions to perform common tasks quickly from anywhere in Fiskl without navigating through multiple menus.

## What Are Quick Actions

Quick Actions provide one-click shortcuts to your most frequent tasks. Instead of navigating to a specific section and selecting multiple buttons, you can open the relevant form immediately from the top-right corner of any page.

**Quick Actions save time by:**
- Eliminating navigation steps
- Providing consistent access across all pages
- Opening forms in convenient modals
- Remembering your last-used settings

## Accessing Quick Actions

The Quick Actions button appears in the top-right corner of every page:

**To open Quick Actions:**

1. Look for the **+ Quick Actions** button (or just **+** icon on mobile)
2. **Select the button**
3. A dropdown menu appears with available actions
4. **Select the action** you want to perform

The menu stays open until you select an action or click outside it.

## Available Actions

The actions you see depend on your user permissions:

### For Full Account Access

- **Create Invoice** - Generate a new invoice for a client
- **Record Expense** - Log a business expense
- **Log Time** - Track billable or non-billable hours
- **Add Mileage** - Record business mileage for reimbursement
- **Create Multi-Journal Entry** - Record complex accounting transactions
- **Add Client** - Create a new client record
- **Add Vendor** - Create a new vendor record
- **Add Product/Service** - Add an item to your catalog

### For Billing & Invoicing Access

- **Create Invoice**
- **Log Time**
- **Add Mileage**
- **Add Client**

### For Limited Access

- **Log Time**
- **Add Mileage**

:::info
If you don't see an action you need, you may not have the required permission. Contact your account administrator to request access.
:::

## Using Quick Actions

### Creating an Invoice

**Steps:**

1. Select **+ Quick Actions**
2. Select **Create Invoice**
3. The invoice creation form opens in a modal or new page
4. Complete the invoice details:
    - Select a client
    - Add line items
    - Review calculated totals
5. Select **Save** or **Save & Send**

The form remembers your preferences like default tax rates and payment terms.

### Recording an Expense

**Steps:**

1. Select **+ Quick Actions**
2. Select **Record Expense**
3. The expense form opens
4. Complete the expense details:
    - **Select vendor** (or leave blank for one-time expenses)
    - **Enter amount** and currency
    - **Choose category** (ledger account)
    - **Add receipt** (optional but recommended)
    - **Enter description**
5. Select **Save**

The expense appears in your Expenses list and updates your Dashboard metrics immediately.

### Logging Time

**Steps:**

1. Select **+ Quick Actions**
2. Select **Log Time**
3. The time entry form opens
4. Complete the time details:
    - **Select date** (defaults to today)
    - **Enter duration** (hours and minutes)
    - **Choose client** (if billable)
    - **Select service** (what work was performed)
    - **Add notes** (optional but helpful)
5. Toggle **Billable** on or off
6. Select **Save**

Your time entry is available for invoicing (if billable) or internal reporting.

### Adding Mileage

**Steps:**

1. Select **+ Quick Actions**
2. Select **Add Mileage**
3. The mileage form opens
4. Complete the mileage details:
    - **Select date** (defaults to today)
    - **Enter distance** (miles or kilometers based on settings)
    - **Add start/end locations** (optional)
    - **Choose purpose** (client visit, meeting, errand)
    - **Add notes** (optional)
5. Select **Save**

The mileage entry calculates reimbursement automatically based on your configured rate.

## Quick Actions on Mobile

On mobile devices, the Quick Actions button appears as a floating **+** icon:

**To use on mobile:**

1. **Tap the + icon** in the bottom-right corner
2. The actions menu slides up from the bottom
3. **Tap the action** you want
4. The form opens full-screen
5. Complete the form
6. **Tap Save**

The floating button remains accessible even when scrolling, so you can create records without returning to the top of the page.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Speed up Quick Actions with keyboard shortcuts:

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl+I` or `Cmd+I` | Create Invoice |
| `Ctrl+E` or `Cmd+E` | Record Expense |
| `Ctrl+T` or `Cmd+T` | Log Time |
| `Ctrl+M` or `Cmd+M` | Add Mileage |

:::tip
Keyboard shortcuts work from anywhere in Fiskl, not just when the Quick Actions menu is open.
:::

## Quick Actions Best Practices

**Use Quick Actions for speed:**
When you know exactly what you want to create, Quick Actions are faster than navigating through menus.

**Use full pages for complexity:**
For complex invoices with many line items or custom terms, navigate to the full Invoices page. You'll have more screen space and additional options.

**Leverage keyboard shortcuts:**
If you frequently create invoices, memorize `Ctrl+I`. It's faster than reaching for your mouse.

**Keep forms open:**
On desktop, Quick Action modals can stay open while you reference other information. Switch tabs without losing your progress.

**Set defaults in Settings:**
Configure default values for tax rates, payment terms, and categories. Quick Actions inherit these settings, saving time on every entry.

## Common Questions

### Why don't I see certain Quick Actions?

Quick Actions reflect your user permissions. If you can't access a feature in the regular interface, you won't see it in Quick Actions either.

### Can I customize which actions appear?

Currently, Quick Actions are fixed based on your permissions. You cannot add custom actions or reorder them.

### Do Quick Actions work offline?

No. Quick Actions require an internet connection to save data to your Fiskl account.

### Can I use Quick Actions in bulk?

No. Quick Actions create single records at a time. For bulk operations like importing multiple invoices, use the Import feature in the relevant section.

### Will my draft save if I close the modal?

It depends on the action:
- **Invoices**: Auto-save as drafts
- **Expenses**: Not saved unless you select Save
- **Time/Mileage**: Not saved unless you select Save

Always select **Save** before closing to ensure your data is retained.
