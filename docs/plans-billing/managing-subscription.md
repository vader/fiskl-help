# Manage Your Subscription

This guide explains how to manage your Fiskl subscription and helps you upgrade plans, view billing history, and control team member access.

## Accessing Subscription Management

Manage your subscription from the [Subscriptions & Billing](https://my.fiskl.com/subscription-billing) page. This page contains four tabs: Plans, History, User Changes, and Billing.

:::info
Only users with the Business Owner role can see all four tabs and make subscription changes.
:::

## Plans Tab

The Plans tab displays available subscription tiers and allows you to upgrade or downgrade your current plan.

### Upgrading Your Plan

You can upgrade your plan at any time if you're currently on the Free, Solo, or Pro plan. Upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated billing.

**Steps:**

1. **Navigate to the pricing page**

   From **Subscriptions & Billing**, select the **Plans** tab.

2. **Review the plan options**

   Compare features and pricing for each tier. Any active free or discounted introductory offers will be honored.

3. **Select your new plan**

   Select **Upgrade to [plan name]** for your desired tier.

4. **Confirm the upgrade**

   Review the pro-rated charge and confirm your selection.

Your account upgrades instantly with immediate access to new features. You'll be charged a pro-rated amount for the remaining days in your billing cycle.

### Downgrading Your Plan

If you're on the Solo or Pro plan, you can downgrade to a lower-tier plan at any time.

**Steps:**

1. **Go to the pricing page**

   From **Subscriptions & Billing**, select the **Plans** tab and locate your current plan.

2. **Find the lower tier**

   Locate the plan you wish to downgrade to (the previous tier).

3. **Select Downgrade**

   Select **Downgrade** in the column of your desired plan.

4. **Confirm your choice**

   Review the changes and confirm your downgrade.

Your downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You'll continue to have access to your current plan's features until then.

:::tip
Before downgrading, review the features of each plan to ensure the lower tier meets your business needs. You can cancel the scheduled downgrade at any time before the billing period ends.
:::

## History Tab

The History tab displays your complete billing history with date range filtering.

**Using the History tab:**

1. **Select a date range**

   Use the date picker to filter invoices by time period.

2. **View invoice details**

   Select any invoice to open the detailed view.

3. **Download invoices**

   Use the print function to save invoices as PDF files for your records.

:::tip
If you set your tax number in **Company Settings**, it automatically appears on all invoices.
:::

## User Changes Tab

The User Changes tab shows the complete history of team members on your account. This includes:

- When users joined your team
- When users were removed from the team
- User role assignments and changes
- Date stamps for all user modifications

This audit trail helps you track team composition over time.

## Billing Tab

The Billing tab displays your current plan details and payment method. It contains two cards: Plan Card and Saved Card.

### Plan Card

The left card shows:
- Your current plan name and tier
- Monthly or yearly price
- Any active discounts or offers
- Next billing date

**Managing your plan:**

The top right of the card displays either an "X" or a round arrow icon:

- **X icon**: Cancels your subscription at the end of the current period. You'll revert to the Free plan with limited features.
- **Round arrow icon**: Appears if you've canceled but are still in the current billing period. Selecting this reverts the cancellation and continues your subscription.

### Saved Card

The right card displays your current payment method. Use this area to update card information when:
- Your card is expiring soon
- You want to change payment methods
- You need to update billing address details

Learn more in [Update Billing Information](/plans-billing/updating-billing-info).

## What You Can Control

Your subscription management capabilities include:

- **Plan Selection**: Change tiers based on business needs
- **Billing Frequency**: Choose monthly or yearly billing
- **Team Size**: Add or remove users as needed
- **Payment Method**: Update card details anytime
- **Cancellation**: Stop renewal while maintaining access
