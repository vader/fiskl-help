# Apply Offer Codes

This guide explains how to apply offer codes to your Fiskl subscription and helps you maximize savings with promotional and partner discounts.

## Understanding Offer Codes

Fiskl provides introductory offers when you register and occasionally offers special partner codes with unique conditions. These codes provide discounted rates for specific periods or special terms managed by partners.

## How to Apply Offer Codes

You can apply offer codes in three ways:

### Method 1: During Registration

1. **Go to the registration page**

   Visit the [Fiskl registration page](https://my.fiskl.com/registration).

2. **Select the offer code link**

   Select the **Have an offer code?** link in the top right corner.

3. **Enter your code**

   Type or paste your offer code and select **Apply**.

4. **Complete registration**

   Proceed with your registration. The discount applies automatically.

### Method 2: Using a Special URL

Some partners provide direct registration links with embedded offer codes:

```
https://my.fiskl.com/registration?code=your-offer
```

When you use these URLs, the offer applies automatically without manual entry.

### Method 3: In User Settings

If you already have an account, you can apply certain offer codes through settings:

1. **Open User Settings**

   Select your profile icon and choose **User Settings**.

2. **Go to Account Settings**

   Select the **Account Settings** tab.

3. **Enter the offer code**

   In the **Offer or Partner Code** field, enter your code.

4. **Apply the code**

   Select **Apply** to activate the discount.

:::tip
Most offer codes work best when applied during initial registration. Some codes may not be available for existing accounts.
:::

## Important Rules About Offers

Understanding these limitations helps you make the most of available discounts:

**Offer stacking is not allowed:**
- You cannot combine multiple offer codes
- If you received an offer at registration, you cannot apply another offer later
- Only one discount can be active at a time

**Offers have expiration dates:**
- All offers automatically expire after their set duration
- You'll be notified before an offer ends
- After expiration, you'll be charged the standard rate for your plan

**Regional and plan restrictions:**
- Some offers apply only to specific regions or countries
- Certain codes work only with particular plan tiers
- Partner codes may have unique terms or conditions

**Billing management:**
- Some partner offers include special billing arrangements
- Partner-managed billing may have different payment schedules
- Review terms carefully for partner-specific codes

## Standard vs Partner Offers

Fiskl offers two types of promotional codes:

**Standard Introductory Offers:**
- Available to all new users during registration
- Typically provide the best overall value
- Managed directly by Fiskl
- Standard terms and conditions apply

**Partner Codes:**
- Provided through specific partnerships or affiliations
- May offer unique benefits or extended terms
- Sometimes include partner-managed billing
- Used for special circumstances or targeted promotions

:::info
Fiskl's standard introductory offers are typically the best available. Partner codes serve special circumstances and may have different terms.
:::

## What Happens When an Offer Expires

When your offer code reaches its expiration date:

1. **Automatic transition**: Your subscription continues at the standard rate for your current plan
2. **Advance notification**: Fiskl notifies you before the offer expires
3. **No service interruption**: Your features and access remain unchanged
4. **Rate adjustment**: Your next invoice reflects the new standard pricing

You can downgrade to a lower-tier plan before the offer expires if the new rate exceeds your budget.
