# Choose Your Fiskl Plan

This guide explains Fiskl's subscription plans and helps you select the right tier for your business needs.

## Available Plans

Fiskl offers three paid plans plus a Free tier:

- **Free**: Basic features for getting started and exploring Fiskl
- **Solo**: Essential tools for solo entrepreneurs and freelancers
- **Pro**: Advanced features for growing businesses with small teams
- **Prime**: Full capabilities for established companies with larger teams

All plans include monthly billing. Yearly billing is available on request.

## How to Select Your Plan

Before you begin, consider your business size, required features, and team member count.

**Steps:**

1. **Visit the pricing page**

   Go to the [Fiskl registration page](https://my.fiskl.com/registration) or access pricing from **Settings** > **Subscriptions & Billing** > **Plans** tab.

2. **Compare plan features**

   Review what each tier includes. Identify must-have features for your business such as multi-currency support, accounting features, or team member limits.

3. **Select your preferred plan**

   Select the **Choose Plan** button for your desired tier.

4. **Complete registration or upgrade**

   Enter your payment details to activate your subscription. Your account activates immediately.

:::tip
Start with the Free plan to explore Fiskl's interface and basic features before committing to a paid subscription.
:::

## Understanding Additional Users

Each plan includes a set number of team members. Additional users beyond your plan limit are billed monthly at the per-user rate shown on the pricing page.

**Flexible team management:**
- Add users anytime without immediate charges
- New user charges are not instant, allowing flexible management
- Removing a user on the same day you add another prevents extra charges
- Pro-rated billing applies for users added mid-cycle

## Offers and Discounts

Many plans include introductory offers with discounted rates for your first billing period. These offers apply automatically when you register.

:::info
Discounts don't always carry over when upgrading plans. Pay attention to the pricing amounts shown during plan changes to understand your new rate.
:::

When your introductory offer expires, you'll be charged the standard rate for your current plan. If you downgrade during an offer period, the new offer rate applies after your current billing period ends.

## What Happens When You Cancel

If you cancel a subscription, you revert to the Free plan at the end of your billing period. You'll maintain access to:

- Web and mobile apps
- Most non-accounting data
- Invoice and expense history
- Client and vendor records

**Limitations on Free plan:**
- No accounting features or Chart of Accounts access
- Banking feeds disconnect after several days
- Limited reporting capabilities
- Reduced feature set compared to paid plans

Your data remains intact. If you re-subscribe later, all information will be available, though you'll need to reconnect banking integrations.
