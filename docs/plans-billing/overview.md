# Plans and Billing

This section explains how to manage your Fiskl subscription and helps you control your plan, billing, and account settings.

## Why Manage Your Subscription

Fiskl offers flexible subscription options that grow with your business. You can upgrade for more features, downgrade to save costs, or pause your subscription entirely.

**Key capabilities:**
- Choose from Free, Solo, Pro, and Prime plans
- Upgrade or downgrade at any time
- Apply discount codes and partner offers
- Update payment methods securely
- Cancel or delete your account when needed

## Key Concepts

Understanding these concepts helps you manage your subscription effectively:

- **Subscription Plan**: Your current tier (Free, Solo, Pro, Prime) determining available features
- **Billing Cycle**: Monthly recurring charge (yearly billing available on request)
- **Pro-rata Billing**: When upgrading mid-cycle, you only pay the difference for remaining days
- **Offer Codes**: Discounts applied at registration or during renewal
- **Account Deletion**: Permanent removal of all data (different from cancellation)
- **Downgrade**: Scheduled plan change that takes effect at the end of your current billing period

## How Subscription Management Works

Managing your Fiskl subscription follows a straightforward process:

1. **Select Your Plan**: Choose the tier matching your business needs based on features and user count.
2. **Manage Billing**: Keep payment information current for uninterrupted service and accurate invoicing.
3. **Adjust as Needed**: Upgrade immediately when you grow, or schedule downgrades for the next billing cycle.
4. **Cancel Anytime**: Stop renewal while keeping access until your current period ends.

Your subscription data appears in the **Subscriptions & Billing** section, which contains four tabs: Plans, History, User Changes, and Billing. Only users with the Business Owner role can access all tabs.
