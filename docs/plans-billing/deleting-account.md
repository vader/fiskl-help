# Delete Account

This guide explains how to permanently delete your Fiskl account and helps you understand the complete data removal process.

## Understanding Account Deletion

Deleting your account permanently removes all your data from Fiskl. This action differs from canceling your subscription, which keeps your data intact.

**What gets deleted:**
- All financial records and transactions
- Invoices, quotes, and estimates
- Client and vendor information
- Banking connections and transaction history
- Reports and saved data
- User settings and preferences
- Company information and branding

:::warning
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered.
:::

## Before You Delete Your Account

Take these steps before proceeding with deletion:

**Export your data:**
1. Download important invoices and reports
2. Export your transaction history
3. Save any financial statements you need
4. Back up client and vendor contact information

**Settle outstanding items:**
1. Collect payment on outstanding invoices
2. Pay any pending vendor bills
3. Complete ongoing projects
4. Notify clients of your account closure

**Review alternatives:**
- Consider canceling your subscription instead if you might return
- Downgrade to the Free plan to maintain access without charges
- Export data regularly even if you're keeping your account

## How to Delete Your Account

Before you begin, ensure you've exported all data you need to keep.

**Steps:**

1. **Open User Settings**

   Select your profile icon in the top right and choose **User Settings**.

2. **Go to Account Settings**

   Select the **Account Settings** tab.

3. **Access the sensitive zone**

   Scroll down to the sensitive operations section.

4. **Select Delete account**

   Select the **Delete account** button.

5. **Confirm deletion**

   Read the warning message carefully and confirm you want to proceed with permanent deletion.

Your account and all associated data are now permanently deleted. You'll be logged out immediately.

## What Happens During Deletion

When you delete your account, Fiskl processes these actions:

**Immediate effects:**
1. **Logout**: You're immediately logged out of all devices
2. **Banking disconnection**: All banking integrations disconnect
3. **Payment integration removal**: Payment provider connections end
4. **Access revoked**: You can no longer log in to your account

**Data removal:**
1. **Financial data**: All transactions, invoices, and reports are deleted
2. **Business data**: Clients, vendors, and products are removed
3. **Personal data**: Your profile and company information is erased
4. **Backups**: Data is removed from all backup systems

**Permanent closure:**
- Your email address becomes available for new registrations
- Your company data cannot be accessed by anyone
- No recovery options exist after deletion

## Account Deletion vs Subscription Cancellation

Choose the right option for your situation:

| Feature | Delete Account | Cancel Subscription |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Data** | Permanently deleted | Kept intact |
| **Access** | Completely removed | Free plan access |
| **Reversible** | No | Yes (can re-subscribe) |
| **Banking** | Disconnected | Disconnects after days |
| **Invoices** | Deleted | Preserved |
| **Best for** | Permanent closure | Temporary break |

:::info
If you're unsure whether you'll need your data in the future, cancel your subscription instead of deleting your account. You can always delete later.
:::

## When to Delete Your Account

Consider account deletion when:

**Business closure:**
- You're permanently closing your business
- You're no longer freelancing or self-employed
- Your company has been acquired or merged

**Switching platforms:**
- You've migrated all data to another system
- You're certain you won't return to Fiskl
- You've completed all outstanding work

**Privacy concerns:**
- You want complete data removal from our systems
- You're required to delete data for compliance reasons
- You prefer not to have any data stored

## Alternatives to Account Deletion

Before deleting, consider these options:

**Cancel subscription:**
- Keep your data while stopping charges
- Maintain access to the Free plan
- Easy to re-subscribe if you return
- Learn more in [Cancel Subscription](/plans-billing/canceling-subscription)

**Downgrade to Free:**
- Access basic features without cost
- Keep your data intact
- No billing or payment required
- See [Choose Your Fiskl Plan](/plans-billing/selecting-plans)

**Export and pause:**
- Download all your data
- Cancel your subscription
- Keep the option to return with a fresh start

## Cannot Undo Account Deletion

Once you delete your account:

- **No recovery**: Data cannot be restored under any circumstances
- **No backup access**: We cannot retrieve deleted information
- **Fresh start only**: You can create a new account with the same email, but it will be completely empty
- **Support cannot help**: Even Fiskl support cannot recover deleted accounts

If you have any doubt, choose cancellation instead of deletion.

## Re-registering After Deletion

If you delete your account and later want to use Fiskl again:

1. **Create a new account**: Register with the same or different email
2. **Start fresh**: Your new account has no historical data
3. **Set up from scratch**: Configure settings, connect banking, and add clients again
4. **No data transfer**: Previously deleted data cannot be restored
