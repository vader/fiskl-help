This guide introduces Fiskl's AI-powered accounting and invoicing platform and helps you find the right documentation for your role and workflow.

## What is Fiskl?

Fiskl is a comprehensive accounting platform designed for small businesses to manage:

- **Invoicing and quotes**: Professional, brand-aligned business communications
- **Expense tracking**: Automated scanning and categorisation
- **Banking**: Real-time sync with over 10,000 banks globally
