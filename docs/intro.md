# Welcome to Fiskl Help

Welcome to your primary resource for Fiskl! This documentation is designed to help you master our AI-powered accounting and invoicing platform.

## What is Fiskl?
Fiskl is a comprehensive accounting platform designed for small businesses to manage:
* **Invoicing & Quotes**: Professional, brand-aligned business communications.
* **Expense Tracking**: Automated scanning and categorization.
* **Banking**: Real-time sync with over 10,000 banks globally.

## How to use this Documentation
To help our support AI and your team find answers quickly, we have organized the site into three pillars:

### 1. Getting Started
Essential steps to set up your company profile, tax settings, and brand identity.

### 2. Core Features
Detailed technical guides on managing your ledger, generating financial reports, and processing payments.

### 3. Settings & Integrations
How to connect Fiskl to your existing bank accounts and third-party tools.
