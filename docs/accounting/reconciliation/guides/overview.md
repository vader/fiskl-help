This section covers the full reconciliation workflow in Fiskl — from matching transactions and resolving discrepancies to generating reports and maintaining audit trails.

## What these guides cover

Each guide focuses on a specific stage or scenario within the reconciliation process:

- **How Fiskl handles reconciliation** — understand automatic matching, bulk processing, and how the reconciliation engine works before you begin
- **Bank account reconciliation** — the complete step-by-step process from setup through finalisation
- **Handling reconciliation discrepancies** — identify and resolve missing transactions, duplicate entries, amount errors, and timing differences
- **Reconciliation best practices** — recommended workflows and habits for consistent, accurate results
- **Reconciliation reports** — generate and export documentation for audit trails and historical review

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reconciliation period** | The date range covered, typically matching your bank statement (e.g. 1–31 January) |
| **Auto-matching** | Fiskl automatically identifies and marks matching transactions between your records and bank statement |
| **Discrepancy** | Any difference between your Fiskl balance and bank statement balance that needs investigation |
| **Outstanding items** | Transactions recorded in Fiskl that haven't yet appeared on your bank statement due to processing delays |
| **Draft reconciliation** | A saved in-progress reconciliation you can return to without losing your work |
| **Reconciliation report** | A document showing all matched transactions, adjustments, and the final reconciled balance |

## Reconciliation workflow

The guides follow this sequence:

1. Select the ledger account and enter the statement date and closing balance
2. Review automatic matches Fiskl identifies
3. Resolve any discrepancies — add missing items, correct errors, handle timing differences
4. Confirm the difference reaches zero
5. Finalise the reconciliation
6. Generate a report for your records

:::tip
If you're reconciling for the first time, start with **How Fiskl Handles Reconciliation** to understand what the system does automatically before working through the **Bank Account Reconciliation** guide.
:::
