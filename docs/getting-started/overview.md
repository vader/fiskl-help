# Getting Started with Fiskl

This section explains how to set up your Fiskl account and helps you get started with invoicing, banking connections, and core accounting features.

## Why Start Here?

Fiskl simplifies financial management for small businesses and accountants. Whether you need to send your first invoice, connect your bank, or set up your Chart of Accounts, these guides provide a clear path to getting your account operational.

**Key benefits of completing setup:**
- Send professional invoices to clients within minutes
- Automate transaction imports from your bank accounts
- Track expenses and income accurately from day one
- Generate financial reports with confidence

## Who These Guides Are For

### Business Owners and Freelancers
Start with invoice basics to begin billing clients immediately. Then connect your bank to automate bookkeeping and reduce manual data entry.

### Accountants and Bookkeepers
Use the accountant-specific guide to set up your practice portal, invite clients, and manage multiple businesses from a single dashboard.

### Teams and Collaborators
Follow the core setup guides to understand how Fiskl organizes financial data. Then configure permissions and workflows for your team.

## Key Concepts

Understanding these concepts is essential for working with Fiskl:

- **Dashboard**: Your central hub showing key metrics, recent activity, and quick actions. This is the page you see after logging in.
- **Chart of Accounts**: The foundation of your accounting system. Fiskl provides pre-configured accounts based on your region and industry.
- **Multi-currency**: Handle transactions in multiple currencies automatically. Fiskl converts amounts to your base currency using current exchange rates.
- **Banking Connections**: Secure links to your financial institutions that automatically import transactions. Fiskl uses Open Banking protocols and never stores your bank credentials.
- **Base Currency**: Your primary operating currency. All reports and totals display in this currency by default.

## How Fiskl Works

Fiskl connects three core areas of your business finances:

1. **Sales and Invoicing**: Create professional invoices, send them to clients, and track payments. Connect payment gateways to accept online payments directly.

2. **Banking and Transactions**: Link your bank accounts to automatically import transactions. Fiskl categorizes them and suggests matches to invoices and expenses.

3. **Accounting and Reporting**: Every transaction updates your Chart of Accounts in real time. Generate financial reports including Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and cash flow statements instantly.

These areas work together to give you complete visibility into your business finances without manual data entry.

## Your Setup Journey

Most users follow this sequence:

1. **Complete your company profile** - Add your business details, logo, and contact information
2. **Configure invoicing** - Set payment terms, customize templates, and connect payment methods
3. **Connect your bank** - Automate transaction imports and reduce manual work
4. **Review your Chart of Accounts** - Ensure accounting categories match your business structure
5. **Create your first invoice or record an expense** - Start using Fiskl with real transactions

You can complete these steps in any order based on your immediate needs. Each guide stands alone and takes 5-10 minutes to complete.

:::tip
If you're migrating from another system, start by connecting your bank and importing historical transactions. This gives you a complete financial picture from day one.
:::
