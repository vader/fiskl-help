This section explains how to set up your accounting practice in the Accountant Portal and helps you configure branches, team members, and client accounts.

## Before You Begin

The Accountant Portal is a complete practice management system designed for accounting firms. The portal has replaced the original single-person portal at `my.fiskl.com/portal` with a new team-based system at `portal.fiskl.com`.

**Key improvements:**
- Team hierarchy with customizable roles and permissions
- Multi-branch support for firms with multiple locations
- Granular access controls per branch and per user
- Flexible billing models (Bill Us or Client Pays)
- Partner referral program integration

## Your Setup Journey

Follow these steps to configure your practice:

1. **Sign up as Super Admin** - Create your parent organization account
2. **Create your first branch** - Set up a branch with currency and payment method
3. **Invite your team** - Add accountants with appropriate permissions
4. **Add clients** - Select billing model and create client accounts

Each step takes 5-10 minutes. Complete setup takes approximately 30 minutes for a basic practice.

## Understanding the Portal Structure

The portal uses a two-tier hierarchy:

**Parent Organization (Top Level):**
- Represents your entire accounting firm
- Manages billing and team access
- Cannot directly invite or manage clients
- Controls all branches

**Branches (Operational Level):**
- Location-specific divisions (e.g., London office, Paris office)
- Each branch operates in a single currency
- Manages client accounts and team members
- Requires own payment method for Bill Us billing

## What You'll Need

Before starting setup:

- [ ] Business information (firm name, address, contact details)
- [ ] Payment method (credit card for Bill Us billing via Stripe)
- [ ] Team member email addresses
- [ ] List of branches you want to create
- [ ] Currency for each branch
