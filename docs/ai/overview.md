# Fiskl AI

Fiskl AI brings intelligent automation and expert guidance to your accounting workflows. From answering questions about features to helping you understand complex accounting concepts, Fiskl's AI capabilities save you time and help you make better financial decisions.

## Why Use Fiskl AI

Manual accounting tasks consume valuable time that you could spend growing your business. Fiskl AI automates routine processes, provides instant answers to your questions, and helps you understand your financial data without needing deep accounting expertise.

**Key benefits:**
- Get instant answers about Fiskl features and accounting concepts
- Communicate in your preferred language with multilingual support
- Access expert guidance without leaving the platform
- Learn accounting principles as you work
- Solve problems quickly with AI-powered assistance

## Key Concepts

Understanding these concepts helps you get the most from Fiskl AI:

- **Fi**: Your AI-powered advisor that answers questions about Fiskl, accounting, and small business management in multiple languages
- **Knowledge Base**: The comprehensive library of Fiskl documentation, tutorials, and accounting principles that Fi draws from
- **Context**: The information you provide in your questions that helps Fi give more accurate, relevant answers
- **Multilingual Support**: Fi's ability to understand and respond in your preferred language automatically

## How It Works

Fiskl AI operates as your on-demand advisor within the platform. When you have questions about features, accounting concepts, or business practices, you interact with Fi through a conversational interface.

1. **Ask Your Question**: Type your question in any supported language. Be specific about what you need help with.
2. **Fi Analyzes**: The AI processes your question against Fiskl's knowledge base and accounting principles to find the best answer.
3. **Get Your Answer**: Fi provides a clear response tailored to your question, with explanations of relevant concepts.
4. **Continue the Conversation**: Ask follow-up questions to dive deeper or clarify any points you need more information about.

Fi maintains context throughout your conversation, making it easy to explore related topics without repeating information. When you switch to a new topic, start a fresh conversation for the most accurate responses.
