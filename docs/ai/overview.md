# Fiskl AI

This section covers Fiskl's AI-powered features. Fi, your built-in AI CFO, analyses your financial data, answers accounting questions, and guides you through tasks — all without leaving the platform.

## What Fi Can Do

Fi handles a broad range of financial and accounting tasks through natural conversation. You can ask questions about your own data, get guidance on accounting concepts, or work through a task step by step.

| Capability | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Financial analysis | Ask questions about your revenue, expenses, and trends using plain language |
| Accounting reports | Run and explore reports through conversation |
| Tax guidance | Get region-specific answers drawn from official tax authorities |
| Help and concepts | Access Fiskl documentation and accounting explanations instantly |
| Guided tutorials | Follow step-by-step walkthroughs for common tasks |
| Multilingual support | Communicate in your preferred language |

## How It Works

When you ask Fi a question, it determines the best way to respond — searching the knowledge base, retrieving live data from your account, running a report, or launching a guided tutorial. Fi maintains context throughout a conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions without repeating background information. Start a fresh conversation when you switch to a new topic for the most accurate responses.

## Key Concepts

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Fi** | Your AI-powered advisor, built into Fiskl |
| **Knowledge base** | The library of Fiskl documentation, tutorials, and accounting principles Fi draws from |
| **Intelligent routing** | Fi's ability to determine the best approach for each question automatically |
| **Regional tax knowledge** | Regularly updated content from tax authorities including HMRC, the IRS, and agencies in Singapore, Australia, and South Africa |
| **Multilingual support** | Fi detects your language and responds with the same level of expertise in each |

## Using Your Own AI Assistant

Fi works inside Fiskl. You can also connect an outside AI assistant, such as Claude, directly to your Fiskl data.

| | Fi | Your own AI assistant |
|---|---|---|
| Where you work | Inside the Fiskl app | In the assistant you already use |
| Setup | None — Fi is built in | Authorise a connection once |
| Best for | Guided help, tutorials, and tax guidance in context | Working across Fiskl and your other tools in one conversation |

To set this up, see [Connect Your AI Assistant](/integrations/public-api/connect-ai-assistant).
