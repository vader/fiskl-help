# Fiskl AI

Fiskl AI brings intelligent automation, financial analysis, and expert guidance to your accounting workflows. From analysing your revenue trends to answering region-specific tax questions, Fiskl's AI capabilities help you make better financial decisions without leaving the platform.

## Why Use Fiskl AI

Manual accounting tasks consume valuable time that you could spend growing your business. Fiskl AI automates routine analysis, provides instant answers to your questions, and surfaces insights from your financial data — all without needing deep accounting expertise.

**Key benefits:**

- Analyse your financial data with natural language questions
- Run accounting reports and explore trends through conversation
- Get region-specific tax guidance drawn from official tax authorities
- Access Fiskl help documentation and accounting concepts instantly
- Follow guided tutorials for common tasks
- Communicate in your preferred language with multilingual support

## Key Concepts

Understanding these concepts helps you get the most from Fiskl AI:

- **Fi**: Your AI-powered advisor that analyses your financial data, answers questions, and guides you through tasks in multiple languages
- **Knowledge Base**: The comprehensive library of Fiskl documentation, tutorials, and accounting principles that Fi draws from. This includes indexed content from official tax authorities for supported regions.
- **Intelligent Routing**: Fi automatically determines the best way to answer your question — whether that means searching the knowledge base, pulling live data from your account, running a report, or guiding you through a tutorial
- **Regional Tax Knowledge**: Fi accesses regularly updated content from tax authorities including the IRS, HMRC, and agencies in Singapore, Australia, and South Africa. Guidance is matched to your region automatically.
- **Multilingual Support**: Fi detects your language and responds accordingly with the same level of expertise

## How It Works

When you ask a question, Fi follows an intelligent process to deliver the best answer.

1. **Understand your question**: Fi analyses your question to determine what you need — a feature explanation, financial insight, data analysis, or step-by-step guidance.
2. **Route to the right approach**: Based on your question, Fi decides the best path. It may search the knowledge base, retrieve live data from your account, run an accounting report, or start a guided tutorial.
3. **Gather and reason**: Fi pulls the relevant information, connects related data points, and builds a clear response tailored to your situation.
4. **Deliver your answer**: You receive a response that may include explanations, data summaries, charts, or actionable recommendations.

Fi maintains context throughout your conversation. Ask follow-up questions to drill deeper into any topic without repeating background information. Start a fresh conversation when you switch to a new topic for the most accurate responses.
