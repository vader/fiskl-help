This page explains how to review, accept, and reject client invitations in Atlas when business owners invite you to access their Fiskl accounts.

## How Client Invitations Work

Client invitations arrive in Atlas through two paths:

- A client signs up using your **Client Pays** invitation link, completes their setup, and an invitation is sent back to you automatically.
- An existing Fiskl user goes to **External Access** in their Fiskl Dashboard and invites you using your branch email address.

In both cases, the client owns the account and pays their own subscription. You review the invitation in Atlas and decide whether to accept.

## Finding Pending Invitations

Pending invitations appear in the Atlas sidebar navigation.

Look for **Client Invitations** below **Clients** in the left sidebar. A badge shows the count of pending invitations. The badge updates in real time as new invitations arrive and disappears when no invitations are pending.

To view all pending invitations:

1. Go to the sidebar navigation in Atlas
2. Select **Client Invitations** under the **Clients** section
3. All pending invitations display in a list

## Reviewing an Invitation

Each invitation shows details about the client and their request. You can see the client's company name, the business owner's name and email, the date the invitation was sent, and the branch it is assigned to.

If the client included a message when sending the invitation, it appears here as well. Review all details before accepting or rejecting.

## Accepting an Invitation

Accept an invitation to establish the accountant-client relationship and gain access to the client's account.

1. Go to **Client Invitations** in the sidebar
2. Review the invitation details
3. Select **Accept Invitation**
4. Confirm the action

After you accept:

- The client appears in your branch's **Clients** list immediately
- You gain accountant access to the client's account
- The client receives an email notification confirming your acceptance
- You can begin working in their account straight away
- The client continues to pay their own subscription

Either party can disconnect the relationship at any time.

## Rejecting an Invitation

Reject an invitation when you cannot take on the client or the relationship is not a good fit.

1. Go to **Client Invitations** in the sidebar
2. Select the invitation to review
3. Select **Reject Invitation**
4. Select a reason for rejection
5. Add an optional message to the client
6. Select **Confirm Rejection**

After you reject:

- The client receives an email notification with your reason
- The invitation is removed from your pending list
- No relationship is established
- The client can seek another accountant

:::tip
Respond to invitations within 48 hours. Prompt responses — even rejections — leave a professional impression and help clients move forward.
:::

## After Accepting a Client

Once you accept an invitation, you can begin working in the client's account. Common first steps include reviewing their company details and tax settings, checking their Chart of Accounts, connecting bank accounts if needed, and assigning team members to the client.

For details on managing the ongoing relationship, see [Client Connections](client-connections.md).

## Common Issues

<details>
<summary>Invitation not appearing in Atlas</summary>

Refresh the Atlas page and check the **Client Invitations** section again. If the invitation still does not appear, verify with the client that they sent the invitation to the correct branch email address. Invitations sent to an individual accountant's email may route differently.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Accidentally rejected the wrong invitation</summary>

There is no undo for a rejected invitation. Contact the client directly, explain the situation, and ask them to resend the invitation from **External Access** in their Fiskl Dashboard.

</details>
