This section explains how Atlas is structured and helps you get your practice operational from sign-up to your first client.

## What You Need to Do

Setting up Atlas takes four steps. Most practices complete this in under an hour.

1. **Set up your practice** — Sign up and become the Super Admin of your organisation. Your organisation is the top level of your Atlas workspace.
2. **Configure your first branch** — Create a branch and set its billing currency. Branches are where your day-to-day work happens: managing clients, billing, and your team.
3. **Understand your team structure** — Atlas uses two levels of roles: organisation-wide roles and branch-level roles. Knowing this helps you invite the right people with the right access.
4. **Add your first clients** — Choose the billing model that fits each client relationship and connect them to your branch.

:::tip
Atlas is free for accountants. You only pay when you use the Charge Us billing model to manage client subscriptions on behalf of your firm.
:::
