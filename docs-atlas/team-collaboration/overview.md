This section explains how to manage your accounting team in Atlas, from inviting new members to configuring their roles and permissions across branches.

## How Team Management Works

Team management in Atlas is branch-based. You access it from **Team Management** in the left sidebar, which contains two sections: **Team Members** and **Team Invitations**. The branch selector at the top of the menu controls which branch you are viewing.

Each team member is assigned to one or more branches with a role on each. Roles provide a default set of permissions, and you can customise individual permissions to add or remove specific capabilities.

## Key Concepts

**Branch roles** control what a team member can do within a specific branch. Atlas provides three roles: Owner, Admin, and Member. Each role is a permission group — a predefined set of permissions that you can customise per team member.

**Parent roles** provide organisation-wide access across all branches. There are three parent roles: Super Admin, Billing Admin, and Viewer. A team member can have a parent role in addition to their branch roles, or no parent role at all — meaning they only have access to branches they are explicitly assigned to.

**Permissions** are the individual capabilities that make up each role. Atlas has eight permission categories: Organisation, Billing, Team, Parent Roles, Clients, Access Control, Audit & Reports, and Security. You can override any permission for any team member at the branch level.

**Self-documenting UI** — Atlas always shows action buttons even if you do not have the required permission. A tooltip on the button explains which permission is needed. This makes it easy to understand what permissions are required without referring to documentation.

## What You Can Do

From the team management area, you can:

- Invite new team members to a branch or all branches
- Assign and change branch roles (Owner, Admin, Member)
- Customise individual permissions per branch
- Grant or revoke parent roles (Super Admin, Billing Admin, Viewer)
- Manage client access assignments for team members
- Add team members to additional branches
- Reset MFA for team members who have lost access
- Track invitation status and history
