# Fiskl Atlas

Fiskl Atlas is the accountant portal built for bookkeepers and accounting professionals who manage multiple client accounts through Fiskl.

:::info Coming soon

Full Atlas documentation is on the way. In the meantime, here is an overview of what Atlas offers and how to get started.

:::

## What is Atlas?

Atlas gives accounting professionals a dedicated workspace to manage their clients' Fiskl accounts from a single portal at [atlas.fiskl.com](https://atlas.fiskl.com). You can onboard clients, assign team members, manage billing, and monitor your entire practice from one place.

## Key features

- **Client management** — Invite clients, accept invitations, and control access levels across all your client accounts
- **Flexible billing models** — Choose between client-pays and bill-us models, and switch between them as needed
- **Team collaboration** — Manage team members, assign roles and permissions, and organise work across branches
- **Practice reporting** — View your practice dashboard, client portfolio, billing reports, and team performance
- **Partner program** — Join the Fiskl partner program to access benefits, resources, and referral tracking

## Getting started

To access Atlas, visit [atlas.fiskl.com](https://atlas.fiskl.com) and sign in with your Fiskl account. If you do not yet have access, contact your account administrator or [reach out to our team](https://fiskl.com/contact).
