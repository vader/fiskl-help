The Fiskl Global Partner Program lets you earn recurring revenue by referring clients to Fiskl. This page explains how the program works, what you earn, and how to get started.

## How the Program Works

When you refer a client who subscribes to Fiskl, you earn a percentage of their subscription fee as recurring commission. Fiskl uses Partnero as the third-party platform that manages referral tracking, tier progression, and payouts.

The program applies to Client Pays accounts only. Charge Us clients do not generate referral commission because your firm already manages their billing directly.

## Commission Tiers

Your commission rate increases as you refer more clients. Fiskl calculates your tier based on the total number of referred clients across your account.

| Tier | Clients | Revenue Share |
|------|---------|---------------|
| Vibe | 1–50 | 20% |
| Epic | 51–250 | 30% |
| Titan | 251–500 | 40% |
| Legend | 500+ | 50% |

Commission is recurring — you earn your share for up to five years per referred client, not as a one-time payment.

## What Each Tier Includes

**Vibe (1–50 clients)** — Access to the global partner network, basic directory listing, and co-branded marketing resources to help you onboard your first clients.

**Epic (51–250 clients)** — Priority API access, cross-pollination referrals, and an expanded directory profile.

**Titan (251–500 clients)** — A dedicated account manager, enhanced API capabilities, and early access to beta features.

**Legend (500+ clients)** — Strategic partnership status, custom integrations, and the opportunity to contribute to the Fiskl product roadmap.

## Payouts

Fiskl processes payouts monthly through Partnero in USD. Once you meet the minimum payout threshold, earnings are sent directly to your bank account. You can track payout history and pending amounts from the Partnero dashboard.

## Getting Started

To join the partner program:

1. Go to the **Partner Program** section in Atlas
2. Select **Join Partner Program**
3. Complete the registration through Partnero
4. Once approved, set up your referral links from within Atlas

For full details on setting up and managing your referral links, see [Managing Referrals](managing-referrals.md).

:::tip
You can also join the program directly at [partner.fiskl.com](https://partner.fiskl.com/register) and link your account to Atlas afterwards.
:::
