This page explains what the parent dashboard shows and how to use it to monitor your entire organisation across all branches from a single view.

## Accessing the Parent Dashboard

The parent dashboard is available to team members who hold a parent role such as Super Admin, Billing Admin, or Viewer. If you have a parent role, a **View Parent Dashboard** option appears on your branch dashboard. Select it to switch to the organisation-wide view.

## Dashboard Cards

The parent dashboard displays four summary cards at the top of the page.

### Total Branches

This card shows the total number of branches in your organisation along with their health status. Use this to quickly identify whether any branch needs attention.

### Total Clients

This card shows the combined count of clients across all branches in your organisation.

### Total Team Members

This card shows the total number of team members across all branches, broken down by role. You can see at a glance how many Owners, Admins, and Members make up your organisation.

### Total Estimated Bill

This card shows the combined estimated bill across all branches. This gives you a single figure for your organisation-wide Charge Us billing. The same mid-cycle prorating rules apply here as on individual branches — see [Branch Dashboard](branch-dashboard.md) for details on how prorating works.

## Branch Overview

Below the summary cards, the branch overview section lists each branch individually. For every branch you can see:

- The number of active clients
- The number of team members
- A direct link to access the branch

This section gives you a quick way to compare branches and navigate to any branch that needs attention.

## Billing Overview

The billing overview section provides a consolidated view of your financial position across the organisation.

This section shows any unpaid invoices and the next estimated bill for each branch. A summary row at the bottom displays the combined total across all branches and a count of all active subscriptions.

Use this section to identify overdue payments and forecast upcoming costs across your firm.

## Cross-Branch Activity

The bottom section displays the cross-branch activity log. This is a unified audit trail that captures actions happening across every branch in your organisation.

You can use this log to monitor team activity organisation-wide and drill deeper into specific events. This is useful for compliance, oversight, and identifying patterns across your practice.
